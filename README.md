# 🧭 north star

**clarity for the obsessed.**

**north star** is a minimalist ios app for ambitious creatives, founders, and operators who feel pulled in too many directions.  
it helps you align your daily actions with your highest priorities—without bloat, noise, or productivity porn.

---

## 🔥 core concept

you don’t need more tasks.  
you need *direction*.

**north star** centers your life around 3 **pillars**—your core missions—and builds a daily ritual that keeps you aligned with them.

---

## ✨ features

- define 3 **pillars** (long-term identity-defining goals)  
- set a **north star** (the current priority under each pillar)  
- simple, no-clutter **daily check-in**  
- visualize **alignment** over time  
- reminders to refocus when you drift  

---

## 🎯 why it matters

most people are busy.  
few are aligned.

**north star** doesn’t track what you *did*—it tracks whether you *stayed true*.  
this is not for productivity addicts.  
this is for the obsessed.
